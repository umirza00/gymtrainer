# GymTrainer: Workout Log

A free, community-built workout tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. Plan programs, log sets with a rest timer, track progress, body measurements and nutrition. All data stays on your device.

## Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: **mirzaumt@gmail.com**

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?** No. There is no login; everything is stored on your device.

**How do I use the AI features?** AI Meal Scan and AI Coach are optional. Get a free API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey), then paste it in *Settings → Nutrition AI*.

**How do I back up my data?** *Settings → Backup & Restore → Export* saves a JSON file you can import later.

**Apple Health permissions?** Manage them anytime in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → GymTrainer.
